We may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience and analyze traffic. We also use trusted third-party services including:

Log Files

Like most websites, we follow standard procedures of using log files. This includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, referring/exit pages and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

ToolsHub does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided such information on our website, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.
